## Interview guide (10 minutes per student)

1. Course and year. How many courses this semester?
2. "Show me where your saved study material is right now." (Telegram, bookmarks, folders? count them)
3. "Last time you needed something you saved, how did you find it? How long did it take?"
4. Hand them the live app: sign up, add 3 links of their own, then search for one by what it was about. Watch silently, note where they get stuck.
5. "What was confusing? What is missing for you to use it before the session?"
6. "Would you put your exam material here instead of Telegram? Would you pay 49 UAH for exam season?"
7. Ask permission to quote them and to check their usage in a few days.
